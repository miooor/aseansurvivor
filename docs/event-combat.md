# Johor elite combat patterns

Canonical scope: [spec #8](https://github.com/miooor/aseansurvivor/issues/8), [ticket #9](https://github.com/miooor/aseansurvivor/issues/9). This extends the existing optional encounters and leaves the earlier Johor PR workflow separate.

Rescue keeps six captors: one Armoured stalker, two ordinary stalkers and three pocong. Lantern Ward keeps 300 HP and three waves: four pocong; two ordinary stalkers and four pocong; one Armoured stalker, two ordinary stalkers and five pocong. Every actor is encounter-owned and placed through existing collision-valid spawning. Ward enemies target the ward; its damage contract remains 10 contact damage per enemy per simulation second. Fan bolts aim toward that objective and use existing hostile-projectile collision against the player; they do not introduce another ward-HP damage channel.

Armoured stalkers alternate charge → fan → charge. Charge warns for 0.65 seconds, locks aim at warning start, then rushes for 0.5 seconds. Fan warns while stationary for 0.8 seconds and fires five indicated rays at -30, -15, 0, 15 and 30 degrees from the locked aim. Each bolt has speed 190, radius 7, damage 10 and lifetime 2.5 seconds. Each action exposes the existing 1.5-second vulnerability window on completion. Existing health/contact scaling and armour reduction remain.

Briarback retains its normal 3.5-second charge cadence and thorn patches. At or below 50% HP it enters the visibly labelled ENRAGED phase once, caps the current remaining skill interval at 2.6 seconds, then uses 2.6-second intervals. The first newly started enraged action is a burst, followed by charge → burst. An action already warning or rushing completes unchanged. A burst warns while stationary for 0.9 seconds with eight evenly spaced rays, then fires eight speed-150, radius-7, damage-8 bolts with 2.5-second lifetimes. Existing thorn cap five, 0.6-second safe warning, four-second lifetime and shared one-second damage cooldown remain in `hunt.js`.

Warnings use stored aim, matching their eventual projectile or rush direction. Elite rushes retain that direction even when navigation would otherwise steer toward the moved target or fear would steer away from it, including the final rush tick; collision still stops them at terrain. Fear continues to expire on simulation time, and ordinary enemies retain fear steering. Warning rays and fan/burst projectiles share the degree offsets in `src/content/encounters.js`. Existing stun pauses attack progression and slow halves movement. All timers use simulation time and freeze during pause and overlays. Two-line elite labels name CHARGE, FAN, BURST and ENRAGED; static warning rays remain visible under reduced motion. Encounter projectile ownership lets the existing abandon/defeat/victory/menu cleanup remove hazards. An active event blocks raid entry; after abandonment its hazards cannot enter the raid. New games create fresh actors and arrays.

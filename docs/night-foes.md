# Regional rare enemies

Each enemy has one signature ability or passive. Each stage has a four-foe roster that opens at 0:29, 0:51, 1:10 and 1:36. Defeating current rares makes room for later arrivals. Regular campaign waves can introduce a rare every 14 seconds, with two living originals at a time.

## Death effects

| Inspiration | Enemy | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volatile Flameblood | Arang Bara | **Runaway Ember.** On death, a hot coal chases briefly and locks in place before its fire burst. |
| Soul Conduit | Gong Jiwa | **Last Peal.** On death, a gong warns before reviving up to three nearby freshly fallen ordinary ghosts at partial health. Revivals yield a single set of rewards across both lives. |
| Fractured | Bijih Retak | **Tin Shatter.** On death, two smaller tin shards break away. Both shards have ordinary contact attacks. |
| Final Gasp | Kafan Penunggu | **Last Vigil.** On death, a spectral echo chases briefly and can still hurt by contact. Move away until it fades. |

## Utility

| Inspiration | Enemy | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soul Eater | Kantung Roh | **Ember Appetite.** Gains movement speed and damage when nearby ghosts die, up to five stacks. |
| Nullifier | Pelita Hampa | **Wick Snuff.** An extinguishing ring removes a shrine blessing and Kopi on a damaging hit. |
| Shroud Walker | Musang Kabut | **Behind the Haze.** Blinks onto a marked, walkable spot beyond the target. |
| Proximity Shield | Kepah Benteng | **Shell Radius.** Direct player attacks from outside the blue shell ring are blocked. |
| Inner Treasure | Dulang Harta | **Tin Cache.** Drops the exclusive **Dulang Timah** relic once per run. Collect it to equip +15% skill damage and +25% pickup range. |

## Debuffs and curses

| Inspiration | Enemy | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corrupting Blood | Getah Merah | **Clinging Sap.** Attacking the sap spirit builds up to three short damage-over-time stacks. Space out your hits. |
| Vile Touch | Ular Bisa | **Venom Touch.** Damaging contact inflicts poison. |
| Hexfont | Batik Sumpah | **Batik Hexes.** A sequence of seals alternates chill, damage reduction and snare by cast. |
| Vine Snare | Rotan Jerut | **Rattan Tether.** A marked lane snares a survivor briefly on a damaging hit. Dashes still move at their own speed. |
| Bringer of Weakness / Enfeeble | Topeng Layu | **Withering Mask.** A diamond-shaped hex reduces outgoing damage by 25% for 2.5 seconds on a damaging hit. |

## Elements and thorns

| Inspiration | Enemy | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical Thorns | Kulit Durian | **Durian Spines.** Returns a capped part of direct physical hit damage. |
| Elemental Thorns | Kumbang Pualam | **Prism Carapace.** Returns a capped part of direct elemental hit damage. |
| Storm Herald | Guruh Bukit | **Hill Thunder.** Three staggered lightning strikes line up around the target. |
| Light Bolt | Wau Bulan | **Moon Needle.** A fast, narrow spirit bolt travels along the warning lane. |
| Inferno Bolt | Bara Rimba | **Canopy Ember.** A slower fire bolt applies a short burn on a damaging hit. |
| Snow Bolt | Kabut Cameron | **Highland Rime.** A frost bolt chills on a damaging hit. |
| Lightning Mirage | Beruk Kilat | **Thunder Double.** Taking a direct hit creates a pursuing double. It stops before detonating. |

The abilities adapt the requested modifier concepts to ASEAN Survivor. Ground warnings show where attacks will land, and damaging hits respect the survivor’s defenses. The roster and sprites are original. [Path of Exile’s monster modifier design notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3323944)

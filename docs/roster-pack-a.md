## Scope and failure cases

Owned production files are `src/game/heroes/{asha,mira,dani}.js` and matching `src/scenes/heroes/*.ts`; owned fixtures are `tests/hero-{asha,mira,dani}.{html,js}`. Shared registration, successful-cast wrapping, damage ordering, audio, assets, flags and scene dispatch remain foundation/integration responsibilities. No save migration or new dependency is needed.

- Asha: Q must refresh existing tickets before nearest fill and cap globally at three. Basic must choose nearest unserved in range, serve only an accepted hero basic hit, and reject oil-slip/item damage. E snapshots and removes served/in-range tickets before bursts; overlap may hit once per collected ticket, but death and expiry grant no free burst. Base E remains useful. R ticks exactly at seconds 1–5, serving existing tickets only. Boss crowd control remains guarded.
- Mira: one collision-valid endpoint expires at five seconds. Only continuous ordinary ground movement within inclusive distance 60–300 earns absolute 90-degree angular displacement; wiggles do not accumulate. Outside range, synthetic jumps and dash rebase progress and starting position. Ready E hits a real triangle plus 35-unit edge margin, once per enemy; base E works without readiness. F reduces incoming damage by 25% for three seconds. R captures origin/aim and makes exactly three fixed strikes at .3/.7/1.1 seconds.
- Dani: E replaces a six-second anchor. Only ordinary movement reaching 160 distance arms it; return within 70 and F consumes it. F outside range keeps base damage/slow and retains anchor. Return grants a separate nonstacking 30-HP/four-second shield and 35-damage anchor pulse. Mitigation precedes absorb, absorb precedes lethal/Last Lantern, and `p.shield` immunity remains separate. R ticks exactly at seconds 1–6 and provides 30% reduction only while inside its radius-200 field.
- Every hero: rank scaling uses `rm` damage and ordinary `rc` cooldowns; all five casts have usable names/captions and AUTO. Rejected cooldown/ultimate/basic casts change no resource, pose, audio or cast-hook count. First level-up produces choices with empty EVOS. All eighteen equipment options must equip without failure, with all six skill supports and Split Ember checked for item-origin isolation. Paused/upgrade/equipment overlays freeze timers. Menu/defeat/retry/new-run and both raid transitions clear only owned fields/projectiles/schedules; first cast after transition survives. Schedules are bounded at twelve or authored smaller caps.
- Visual/input: distinct persistent ticket, tow/triangle, anchor/shield/field geometry must match simulation and remain legible through shape/text with audio muted/reduced motion. Inspect each real hero with selected art, keyboard and simultaneous touch movement/cast, and 320/390 widths. Download timestamped JSON and screenshots as repeatable evidence.
